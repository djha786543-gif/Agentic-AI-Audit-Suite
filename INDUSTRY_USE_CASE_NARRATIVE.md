# End-to-End Industry Use Case: AI Lending Governance For A Regulated Bank

## Use Case Overview
A tier-1 financial institution deploys an agentic AI lending assistant that supports credit decisioning for SME loans.
The internal audit and model risk teams need one continuous assurance workflow that demonstrates:
- AI onboarding governance
- control effectiveness
- evidence traceability
- board and regulator reporting
- ongoing operational hardening

This narrative maps the complete lifecycle to the portal pages so stakeholders can understand exactly how the platform operates in production.

## Industry Scenario
- Industry: Banking / Financial Services
- Business Process: AI-assisted underwriting and credit decision support
- Regulatory Pressure: SOX 404, COSO 2013, model risk governance, internal policy assurance
- Problem: Manual quarterly testing misses drift, exception trends, and policy execution gaps
- Target Outcome: Always-on AI governance with auditable evidence and executive-ready reporting

## End-to-End Workflow Story
### Step 1: Program kickoff and governance objective
The audit lead defines the assurance objective: validate that AI credit decisions remain policy-aligned, explainable, and controlled.

Portal modules:
- `index.html`: executive framing, architecture context, and workflow orientation
- `help.html`: operating guidance, process steps, and role-based usage instructions

Outcome:
- Scope, stakeholders, and expected control coverage are aligned before data onboarding.

### Step 2: AI system onboarding and control setup
The team onboards lending datasets and initializes agent workflows for control testing.

Portal modules:
- `app.html`: primary command center for data load, agent orchestration, and run execution
- `settings.html`: runtime and environment-level configuration controls

Outcome:
- The AI use case is registered with clear parameters, test boundaries, and operating assumptions.

### Step 3: Control testing and transaction validation
Autonomous agents execute full-population tests across access, change, transaction, and fraud-related control domains.

Portal modules:
- `app.html`: central execution and findings navigation
- `itgc-controls.html`: IT general control checkpoint surface
- `itac-testing.html`: IT application control checkpoint surface

Outcome:
- Exceptions are detected with higher coverage than sample-based testing.

### Step 4: Evidence generation and audit traceability
Every control result, event, and integrity marker is preserved for challenge and replay.

Portal module:
- `vault.html`: evidence vault with artifact persistence and traceability context

Outcome:
- The institution gains defensible, review-ready evidence for internal and external audit.

### Step 5: Risk and policy governance oversight
Detected exceptions are connected to policy obligations, risk treatment, and alert workflows.

Portal module:
- `governance.html`: policy inventory, framework mapping, alerts, and risk register oversight

Outcome:
- Governance decisions become operational and measurable, not just documentation.

### Step 6: Executive and regulator reporting
The audit function produces board-ready reporting packages and trend-focused assurance outputs.

Portal module:
- `reports.html`: executive summary, findings report, compliance status, KPI dashboard, and risk register exports

Outcome:
- Leadership sees assurance posture, residual risk, and remediation status in one place.

### Step 7: UAT hardening and continuous readiness
The engineering and audit teams run readiness gates and patch workflows to keep the platform stable as conditions evolve.

Portal module:
- `uat.html`: UAT run control, readiness evaluation, phase2 planning, patch generation/apply, and autopilot modes

Outcome:
- The platform sustains reliable, industry-ready operation over time, not only at go-live.

## Enterprise Value Proposition
Organizations implement this platform because it:
- Reduces AI governance risk by shifting from periodic sampling to continuous, full-population control testing
- Improves auditability through structured evidence lineage, reproducible artifacts, and immutable decision trails
- Accelerates compliance reporting with pre-assembled board and regulator-ready outputs
- Enables responsible scaling of agentic AI by combining governance, controls, and operational hardening in one lifecycle
